**Rule.** A movable thing in the street the player can hit (a bin, a cone, a café chair / table, a Villo bike in its dock, a parked motorbike / scooter, a sign post, a bollard) is a PROP, not a wall: a 3D rigid body (its drawn geometry's box, its class's mass and inertia, contact points of its shape: a parked motorbike stands on its tyres + stand, tips over and lies on its side) woken on contact near the player only (a fixed pool of 24 awake bodies, 120 Hz substeps only while one is awake, asleep when settled, zero allocation per step), ONE impulse with BOTH masses at the contact point (the vehicle keeps the plan part: it loses speed by the mass ratio), the ground and the drive colliders as for the cars (never ending inside a wall or the ground); a post bends (a plastic hinge resisting with its yield force) and snaps above its speed; what really stops a car (lamps, trees, walls, buildings, Villo docks) stays a wall. A woken prop leaves its pool by one update range and is drawn by fixed-capacity proxies of its pool's own geometry / material / columns (update ranges, never DynamicDrawUsage, no draw per prop, motion vectors); it stays where it lands while its tile is loaded and resets when it unloads; the traffic brakes for it; the walker kicks the light ones; a `propHit` event for the sound.

**Why.** owner playtest 2026-11-18: "we should have more objects we could hit with the car: the parked motorbikes and other things": every bin, sign, bollard and parked motorbike was a static wall (a 1.4 t car at 50 km/h stopped dead by an 18 kg bin), the accident job's cones had no collider at all
