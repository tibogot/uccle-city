**Rule.** A raised street surface (sidewalk, paved island, dropped kerb, tree pit) is level with its kerb: its cross-section follows the kerb top with a real cross-fall (<= 4 % up, <= 2 % down away from the kerb), its long profile follows the street; only a surface that runs on to another street at another level keeps its own ground. The terrain meets a street edge by a signed-distance bank from the EXACT edge (above it a 45 degree cut down to the edge mirrored under it, below it the weld's shoulder and bank), never by a raster (grid-cell) distance. Where the land behind a sidewalk is PRIVATE (not a park / wood / green area, with a garden front or a house behind it) and stands >= 0.4 m over it, the step is a RETAINING WALL (face, coping, the garden's lawn on top), never a bare cut, and every garden piece stands on what is drawn (no void > 5 cm under it). Banks are grass up to ~35 degrees. One wall line per sidewalk run, on its back edge only: never a second wall within 4 m behind another, gaps under 3 m carried across unless a gate stands there (the garden data of the neighbour tiles included: a garden front runs over a tile border); no wall across a garden gate, and a gate over a jump > ~0.3 m gets a flight of steps (risers ~17 cm) between the piers' returns. A wall face reads as its material at the distances it is seen from (bond / courses to ~2 px, then the face's average with its mortar share, never the bare brick colour); the garden behind a wall's lawn rises in a grass bank (<= ~31 degrees) where the cut allows.
