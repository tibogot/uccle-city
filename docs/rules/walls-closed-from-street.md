**Rule.** Every building the tile worker draws is closed from the street AS DRAWN, after every step that moves or adds its walls (wall repair, coplanar clean, wall feet, facade kit): along each outer footprint edge that is not a party stretch, from the drawn ground to the building's own roof, a ray from the open air meets a front-facing wall within 1.2 m behind the edge. A step that moves wall vertices moves only the mesh's LOWER BOUNDARY: a foot is an edge with no wall of the building under it (no triangle on it reaching below its line, no wall triangle holding the point just under either corner); the corners of any other edge stay where the source put them, even when a true foot shares them. An edge the source leaves empty is rebuilt like a partial one (base to the roof just inside it) unless it is a party stretch or a recess. An opening that is real (an arcade, an undercroft) or a source defect we do not fix (UrbIS short / inward walls) is a counted class under a ratchet ceiling, never a raised number without a Street View look. A view eye (tour, shot, billboard street eye) never stands inside a building.

**Why.** Block 520249 (I3) looked like a block with no walls: the billboards eye stood 2.2 m inside it (every wall culled from inside). The census (docs/MISSING_WALLS_CENSUS.md) then found the real class: viewer/groundFit.js sinkFeet called an edge a foot when its two corners were the lowest wall VERTICES at their x|z, and moved every vertex at that x|z|y in every wall triangle: plinth tops under recessed porches (I3 536783), steps in the solid's bottom outline (I3 540548) and porch lintels 2.4 m up (I3 539754) were pulled down and the wall above tore: 40,002 of 288,154 foot edges, 996 buildings, 4,755 m2. And viewer/wallRepair.js skipped any edge with no UrbIS triangle at all (`if (!on.length) continue;`: F7 723572 / 723573, 100 buildings). Nothing saw it: rule faces-out's `shell` row reads the UrbIS solids and the repair, never the walls after the feet; the feet were checked for contact with the ground (tools/groundfit/scan.mjs), never for what they do to the wall above. Switches: `?feet=old`, `?wallempty=old`.
