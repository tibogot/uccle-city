**Rule.** A ridden vehicle falls only on a REAL crash: a head-on impact (within ~53 deg of square) above the vehicle's crash speed into something solid (wall, building, tree, lamp, car, furniture), or a landing far outside its bands; never from steering, braking or the gas (the lean target stays within the grip the surface gives, traction control + cornering ABS take what the lean leaves, a grip loss is a SLIP that recovers), never from a glancing contact, a kerb, a step / bank face under the wall height, cobbles, rails or grass. Air is physical (the body leaves the ground where it falls away faster than gravity) and every touch-down is judged in three bands (clean / sketchy = a wobble and some speed lost / bail). Each vehicle keeps its character (top speed, power, the Villo pushed up steep hills). Proven headless on scripted routes (synthetic + the real city, a keyboard, an analog and a late-braking rider) with every crash logged with its trigger; the old tuning kept behind a `?x=old` switch that fails the same table.

**Why.** owner playtest 2026-11-18: "I tried the motorbike, it's barely usable, the guy always falls". tools/moto/feelTest.mjs on the old tuning: 29 falls on 365 rides that should have none (lean 15: full lean + brake at any speed or + gas under 72 km/h = a low-side 0.35 s later; step 12: the car's old step rule made a steep street, a ramp, a bank or stairs a wall, hit at speed; tree 2), the first city runs with the parked cars on the line also fell at every glancing parked-car touch (any 8.5 m/s INTO anything = a crash), and a 12 m cliff was ridden down as a slope (no air) (claude/moto)
