**Rule.** Every feature of the bike lab's BMX park (viewer/bike/bikePark.js) is RIDDEN by the real physics, proven by a scripted rider at the feature's own speed: the wooden kickers land on their landing ramps, the dirt doubles line is cleared at pump speed with no bail, the pump track and the berm loop are lapped (the berm holding more lean than flat ground), the quarter pipe gives a vert air that comes back into the ramp turned 180, the mini half-pipe goes back and forth (kick turns at the top), the spine transfers, the rails / ledge / handrail are ground, the wall ride rides the wall, the drop-in is rolled in. A steep ramp is a smooth TRANSITION the bike follows (attached: the pitch is the chord between the wheels, the speed carried along it), never a face that stops it; a face is only an ABRUPT rise. The park is procedural JS, one merged ground mesh and one merged tube set, no light added, no DynamicDrawUsage; BIKE stays under ~120 properties (the park's constants are BIKE_PARK).

**Why.** owner 2026-11-25: "the lab should have ramps and other fun things to test the BMX". The two-spring bike could not ride a quarter pipe: the front's normal force, under the CoM, pitched it nose down and lifted it off the curve (it climbed a 70 deg wall at 45 deg and flew onto the deck); the face test (any 2 cm rise steeper than 58 deg) stopped it on every steep transition; every jump left the ramp at the chord's angle with the rear still on it (short, cased); the dirt doubles bled speed until the line died; and BIKE past 128 properties made every step allocate (claude/kid)
