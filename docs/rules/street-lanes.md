**Rule.** On every road the street model covers (a segment with at least one measured station: M / D driving ends, E where UrbIS maps nothing and the drawn road is the engine's own strip, X with measured ends), the traffic lanes ARE the model's lanes: offset curves of the segment's axis (the mid-line of the measured carriageway) at the model's driving-lane centres, smoothed along the whole segment (a running median and two box passes, C1), then held >= 1.0 m inside the measured driving ends AND the measured kerb / street-edge lines, and 1.13 m clear of the parked cars, by ONE smooth offset profile (ramps <= 1:10, gaps under 30 m held: never point by point); on an X station one lane per direction, centred in its half of the measured carriageway; a two-way carriageway narrower than 3.2 m (a one-way one narrower than two clearances) carries one centred lane (the sim's narrow-stretch rule lets one direction through at a time). A segment end at a junction ends at the arm's MEASURED mouth (an unmeasured arm at the engine's trim, never further out); a degree-2 node inside a segment is one continuous curve. A junction turn between two model lanes is a quintic Hermite curve matching both lanes' end tangents and curvatures (G2), its handle chosen to stay inside the junction polygon / floor disc with the lowest peak curvature. viewer/traffic/tileTraffic.js stays the ONE builder for the runtime and every audit; laneShifter, the fitToSurface kerb push / parked-car shift / far snap and roadFollowTurns apply only to roads the model does not cover, and everywhere with `?streetlanes=old`. Every exception to the invariants is a gap-register entry by named cause.

**Why.** The lanes were the engine's OSM-swept lanes pushed sideways station by station (laneShifter after the line fit, the class raster push, the parked-car shifts, a far-lane snap through buildings, turns re-laid along far roads): each push was a local fix and the sum wove (slalom 7.9 km city-wide), cut junction corners and drove through houses where the OSM line was far off the street. The model already holds the measured carriageway and the lane placement the paint is drawn from; lanes laid from it once, as a road system lays them (an offset curve, G2 turns inside the junction), need no correction afterwards (claude/street4, docs/STREET_MODEL_PLAN.md job 4).
