**Rule.** A street is described ONCE, by the street model (viewer/street/model.js), and consumers read it instead of re-deriving the carriageway. A segment is a chain of network roads continued through degree-2 nodes, id `s:<first way>:<nodeA>-<nodeB>`, owned by the tile holding its arc-length midpoint; a junction (every node of degree >= 3) is a polygon through its arms' mouth lines, owned by its node's tile. Every station of every kerb, drive end, axis and lane boundary carries a confidence: E D-engine (see below), M measured (a UrbIS carriageway polygon end ON a measured kerb / street-edge line; a UrbIS Topo / Brussels Mobility lane line), D derived from M (a gap of at most 15 m; lanes between two M driving ends on a width stable within +-0.4 m over >= 30 m), O OSM only, G engine default, X contradiction (the OSM line > 1.5 m outside the measured carriageway; a lane stack that does not fit it). Every element is a pure function of the network, the overrides and the SURVEYED inputs of the tiles its corridor touches, so a neighbour carries a byte-identical copy of the owner's element: no tile rebuilds another tile's element from its own clipped raster. Paint from the model (viewer/street/markings.js): an edge line only as the offset curve 0.30 m inside an M raised kerb (refined onto this tile's drawn kerb within 0.5 m, else none); a lane / centre line only on M / D boundaries, where both kerbs hold within 0.3 m over +-7 m (no taper), the line turns < 4 deg off its axis and never jumps sideways; nothing inside a junction (polygon or floor disc), over a measured crossing, across another segment's carriageway, on O / G / X, or in a run under 6 m Where UrbIS measures nothing at all along a road (no measured carriageway within reach of any station, none X: the Flemish fringe, unmapped private lanes) the drawn road IS the engine's strip from the same OSM line: confidence E ("D-engine"), and that strip keeps its own centre line and edge lines (0.30 m inside its edge), judged against the drawn strip by the same invariants; nothing where UrbIS measures part of the street (`?streetmodel=old` = the step-1 lines on the fitted engine strips).

**Why.** Eight ground functions, five carriageway class sets and per-way fits of the OSM line with a guessed lane stack made every street defect local and every fix a new constant named after a street (docs/STREET_MODEL_PLAN.md 1). The owner's Ch. de Saint-Job / Rue Engeland line (E7) ran diagonally because the lagging OSM stack carried it; at the D7 | E7 seam each tile rebuilt the other's road from its clipped raster and painted it differently. Measured kerbs give the line directly, the seam needs one owner, and a confidence on every station decides where nothing may be drawn (claude/street2).
