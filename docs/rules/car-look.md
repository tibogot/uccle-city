**Rule.** A car's paint is ONE shared material: a dielectric base (metalness 0, roughness 0.6) under a clear coat (factor 1, roughness 0.035); gloss black trim (kind 10) has the coat too, rims are alloy (kind 11), panel gaps (kind 12) are the paint's colour, dark, with no coat. Its REFLECTIONS (coat, base specular, chrome, glass, alloy) come from the cars' reflection probe (viewer/carProbe.js: the far tiles + the sky round the camera, 64 px, one cube face every 3 frames, two cubes cross-faded, a PMREM per completed cube), its DIFFUSE light from the sky's environment as before. The post chain's SSR stays OFF the paint (its mask is the metalness): it is an extra layer only if a moving-camera strip shows no added flicker. Detail goes into LOD0 only (within 30 m); LOD1 and LOD2 never grow. The probe draws a proxy (far tiles), never the scene, and owns one fixed key light in its own scene (made once).

**Why.** CARS LOOK round 1 (owner 2026-11-24, NTE as the bar): the cars read matte and plastic because they reflected the sky only (no street, no buildings) and the paint was 25 % metallic with a weak coat. A probe that rendered the city scene would rebuild a pipeline per city material for its render target (freeze risk) and cost 40-60 draws a face; the far tiles are 2 draws a tile on materials the far ring already compiles. First browser look: with the probe as the WHOLE environment every grey car in a brick street turned bronze (the probe's irradiance and its walls brighter than its sky): diffuse now stays on the sky, the probe sky is x4 and its key x0.5 (matched to the view). SSR on the paint (`?carssr=1`) added still-camera flicker on the cars (second difference 1.009 -> 1.093, hot pixels 0.110 -> 0.121 %, same path, stepped frames) and nothing the probe lacks: rejected (SMOOTH OR NOTHING). The probe itself adds none (still 1.009 vs 1.037 without it). 128 px every 2 frames put the PMREM on 1 frame in 12 (police drive GPU p95 +1-1.8 ms): now 64 px every 3.
