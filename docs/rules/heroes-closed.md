**Rule.** Every hero (landmark GLB) is closed from the street at EVERY LOD: no wall piece missing, no face wound inward. In the generators: every polygon fill with holes is checked before it is triangulated (each hole inside the outer loop, no two holes overlapping: `tools/blender/polycheck.py`, an error at build time), every swept profile faces out (counter-clockwise in its (out, up) frame), wall normals are oriented against the PLAN's centre, never a fitted arc's; a meshopt far level keeps the borders between slots (LockBorder). (round 2, claude/defects3) A far / simplified level is a closed shell too: every opening it cuts has its jambs (a recessed leaf, a set-back pane), a surround or slab standing proud of a wall over an opening has closed edges, two pieces that meet use the same absolute line (never one measured off an axis and the other absolute), and a far level's triangle target is raised until the probe is 0 (Saint-Pierre LOD2 1500 -> 3000). An opening that is seen through by design (an open lantern, a colonnade, a canopy) is listed with its reason; a party wall against a neighbour's solid is not a hole.

**Why.** Saint-Pierre's south side had whole wall pieces missing (owner playtest 2026-11-18, screenshot 88): the porch bay was cut at bay 4 while the porch stands under bay 3, so bay 4's panel got two overlapping holes and `tessellate_polygon` silently returned a broken wall (~50 m2), and the side stopped at u 33.3 where the south-east annex starts, so the wall above the annex's roof was never built (~40 m2). Census: 11 of 16 heroes open. Nothing looked: `shell` / wallAudit read only the UrbIS solids (every hero's solid is hidden), mesh_audit counts a hole wider than 30 mm as an "opening" (info) and has no "faces outward" check, 4 heroes were never pack-audited. Same class found elsewhere: Saint-Augustin's plinth, copings and bands all swept inward (1,116 back-face fails), a Saint-Job corner chapel's walls and roof turned inward (its arc centre fitted outside the church, ~20 m2), the meshopt LOD2 slits between slots; (claude/defects3) Saint-Pierre's far variant (LOD2 source) still left 11.8 m2: its doors' leaves recessed with no jambs and its portal / side-door surrounds as slabs with open edges (grazing rays slid in), the east wall stubs used WALL_S / WALL_N off the axis WC (a 7 cm slit at the south-east corner), and LOD2 at 1500 tris collapsed the south-west corner pilaster
