**Rule.** Every hero (landmark GLB) is closed from the street at EVERY LOD: no wall piece missing, no face wound inward. In the generators: every polygon fill with holes is checked before it is triangulated (each hole inside the outer loop, no two holes overlapping: `tools/blender/polycheck.py`, an error at build time), every swept profile faces out (counter-clockwise in its (out, up) frame), wall normals are oriented against the PLAN's centre, never a fitted arc's; a meshopt far level keeps the borders between slots (LockBorder). An opening that is seen through by design (an open lantern, a colonnade, a canopy) is listed with its reason; a party wall against a neighbour's solid is not a hole.
