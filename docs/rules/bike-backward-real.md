**Rule.** The kid's BMX goes backward only the ways a real BMX does: it freewheels, so it is never pedalled backward. (1) S / Down (gamepad LT) held at a STANDSTILL (under BIKE_FLOW.backStill 0.3 m/s for backDelay 0.25 s, no front brake, no pedal, grounded, the grade under backGrade 15 %) = the kid stands over the bike, both feet on the ground, and walks it back at backWalk (1 m/s): the brake input is his command, no brake force, no torque; A / D steer with the steering geometry reversed (kinematic yaw by the signed speed: the nose swings the other way, the rear goes where the bars point); W, the air or a steep grade end it at once; letting go of S (or squeezing Q) lets the feet stop it softly (backAccel). (2) A bike that stops on a RAMP FACE (grade over fakieGrade 25 %, no brake) rolls back down it - a fakie - steerable, never a crash; S during it is a plain rear brake (no skid backward, no back-walk while it rolls), and back on flat ground a slow fakie is dragged to a stop by the feet (fakieDrag, fading out by fakieDragV so a fast one out of the quarter pipe rolls on). A street hill (to ~20 %) is still held by the foot at a stop. While moving, S stays the rear brake / skid / drift. One key, two meanings by speed: no new key. The lab shows "backing up" / "rolling back" while it lasts and, once per visit after 2 s at a stop, "hold S to back up". The camera never follows a backward velocity (it would look back at itself). ?bikeback=old: none of it.

**Why.** owner 2026-11-28 (bike lab): "we can't go backward; we should be able to, realistically, the way someone goes backward with a BMX" and "S is already used by the drift, isn't it? Be sure the controls are easy, with nice UX." Root cause: the physics had no backward state at all - at a stop with S held the foot-down hold (footHold) zeroed the slope force and damped the speed, so the bike stood forever; and the same hold engaged on any face under 0.6 slope with no brake ("up > -0.03"), so a bike that ran out of speed on the funbox's 25 deg face STUCK on it instead of rolling back. The lab's idle input (mounting / dismounting) held S alone, which would now walk the bike away: it holds both brakes (Q + S = held, never a walk). (claude/bikeback)
