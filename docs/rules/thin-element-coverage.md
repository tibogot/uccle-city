**Rule.** A thin element (a bar, rail, grille, glazing bar: 4V / A under 3 cm) is never drawn as raw geometry where it projects under ~1 internal pixel (1 px = d / 622 m under the TAAU at Auto's floor): beyond that its COVERAGE is drawn instead, pre-filtered (a card whose texture is the bars' exact coverage with box mips, or an analytic box filter), blended, never a hard alpha test at distance, and the card writes depth where it covers (a small depth cut) so the TAAU reprojects it at its own depth (no depth: it smears away while the camera moves); the modelled bars hand over to it by the lodFade dither (no pop), the card fades in under them first, the sun's shadow stays as it was. Every card atlas has a full mip chain that keeps level 0's coverage. A new thin-bar source goes into the census. (round 2, claude/defects3) A hero's thin strokes are counted per hero (jitterCheck `herowires`: the strokes left sub-px without a pre-filter, ceilings); the widen + dither path (viewer/heroWires.js, `?herowire=1`, data off the main thread) measured no gain in the browser and stays opt-in: a fix there must beat `?herowire=1` and the old look in debug/jitterCheck.js before it is on.
