**Rule.** The kid's BMX crashes on a step only from a FRONTAL hit: the front wheel square into a face higher than the axle (BIKE_FLOW.faceCrashH 0.25 m) faster than faceCrash, or the bike's box square into a wall / post / car. Leaving an edge never crashes: rolling off a box, a deck, a kicker's lip or a ramp's side (any height, any speed, square or diagonal) the front wheel drops, the bike lands and rolls on - at worst a sketchy landing. A rule that reads the bike's pose (the endo "front wheel down, rear up = over the bars", the manual's loop-out) waits dropGrace (0.6 s) after a wheel rolled off an edge, judges against level ground at most, and fires only while the nose is still going over (pitchVel under endoBack). A collider that stands for a ground solid's side (a ramp's side panel, a deck's back) is ONE-WAY: it stops a wheel from outside, never one leaving the solid's top. A hop is the bike's own for popDetach after the pop: no ramp-follow overwrites its lift.

**Why.** owner 2026-11-28 (bike lab round 2: "way better"): "crashing INTO a tall obstacle is right, but riding OFF the top of a low obstacle, even over a sharp 90-degree edge, must not crash". Root causes, from a sweep of every solid's top ridden off in 8 directions at 1.5 / 3 / 5 m/s (204 crashes before): (1) the ENDO rule fired as the rear wheel left the edge - the front already down in the gap, the nose 36 deg down vs the landing face's slope ahead = "over the bars" (rolled off the small kicker's lip at 2.2 m/s); (2) the side panels (KIND.wall boxes along each solid's sides, meant to stop a rider beside a ramp) caught a wheel rolling off the top and pushed it back IN: stuck at the edge, or "hit a wall" over 4.8 m/s; diagonally the push came out of a panel piece's end; (3) the rear-led ramp-follow kept a bike that left a ramp's side nose-up, and the loop-out rule read the edge as a steep back slope ("looped out"). The same round's "weak hop on ramps": the transition-follow set vy to the ramp's own every step and swallowed the pop, and a release on a down slope was always a pump. After: 0 crashes off any edge in the sweep (what is left are frontal hits on the next obstacle) (claude/bikelab2)
