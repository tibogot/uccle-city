**Rule.** A lane that passes UNDER a road deck keeps its own street drawn there and its own layer in the drive grid: the deck never stands in for it, never removes it, and the traffic under it never drives on the bare terrain or on the deck's layer. A census of the movers' ground is the CITY's, not a tile's guess: it walks a lane only inside its own tile (past the edge the tile's grid clamps to its border row and has no street), with every deck the city has there (the tile's own and those a neighbour draws over it), and counts only what a mover can stand on: a deck's walked top (not its soffit or its parapets' coping), the drawn street, the tram platforms as tram.js draws them. A road that leaves the city (off every tile's grid) is held at the ground where it last has data, never at a lifted profile.

**Why.** claude/defects1's class 4b (moverground, 2026-11-24): three "traffic under a road deck" spots, D9 (-1033, 2111) -16.8 m, F10 (-458, 2999) +8.7 m, E10 (-755, 2795) -3.4 m, and the Carsoel deck's west end (H6 879, 518) +0.26 m. Only one was what it looked like. D9: the Tilleuls lane ON its own deck, entering D9 from E9; the census walked it from E9 on D9's grid clamped at the border (70 m) and kept that layer under the deck: the census's error (the city's car arrives on E9's ground). E10 -3.4 m: the census's 1.5 m layer chain ran from the service deck's parapet coping through the slab and its soffit down to Square Maas 2.4 m under it, so the "truth" was the coping. H6: the lane crosses the Saint-Job tram platform's west corner, a 25 cm slab tram.js draws and the drive grid holds, which the census did not count as drawn. F10 was real: Kleindalstraat leaves the city at z 3000 into no tile; roadLevels.js dropLifts held that stretch at the engine's lifted profile ("nothing to come down to") and the street stood 8.7 m over its sunken lane at the city's edge, a causeway the traffic drove on (claude/defects2, docs/MOVERGROUND.md).
