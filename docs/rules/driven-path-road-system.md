**Rule.** A driven path is laid as a road system lays it, from the city's own data: (1) a lane keeps ONE lateral offset from its road's alignment and changes it only by a smooth reverse curve (needs from the kerbs / parked cars / build-outs as envelopes, gaps under 30 m held, ramps <= 1:10, C1 blends: viewer/streets/laneOffset.js), never point by point (a lane fitted to its painted lines keeps to its paint, rule 33); (2) a junction turn is an arc inside its junction: a turn end more than 20 m from its node follows its road's alignment to 10 m from it; (3) the moving traffic drives only where the city's carriageway test says cars drive (viewer/streets/carriageway.js NOT_DRIVEN: no service way, alley, private road); (4) where UrbIS measures a road, land outside its street surfaces is never carriageway (the OSM band only where the road itself is unmeasured): the garden kit's walls / railings / hedges on private land stay, they close the front gardens; (5) no plant stands in the drawn carriageway or hangs its crown > 0.15 m over it; (6) a painted line follows the measured carriageway's trend, never the OSM line's angle (on a road shorter than two median windows: a robust local line, the slope cap 1:5). (7) one builder: the worker and every audit build a tile's traffic graph through viewer/traffic/tileTraffic.js with the same inputs (an audit that checks another lane than the one driven is a gap); a lane far off its OSM line through a building goes onto the measured carriageway only where that creates no new building contact. Checked city-wide on the built graph (in the road frame), on the cars' own poses in the running sim, and on what is drawn.

**Why.** owner playtest 2026-11-18 round 2: traffic slalomed (lanes shifted point by point round every parked car and back into every gap: a 1-1.7 m zigzag every 10-20 m), a car left the Chaussée de Waterloo through a house (a 157 m turn connector cut straight across a block from a service alley the traffic should never have used), a line ran diagonally across the lane on the Avenue du Prince de Ligne (the line fit's running median held the OSM line's angle on a short road), bushes stood on the Chaussée de Waterloo, the car drove onto front lawns on Rue Papenkasteel (the OSM band, 1-6 m past the measured street, called the gardens carriageway and main.js dropped their walls and hedges). Nothing counted the paths' smoothness, the traffic in buildings, the plants on the road or the garden pieces the band dropped
