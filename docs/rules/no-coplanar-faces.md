**Rule.** No two drawn faces facing the same way lie in one plane over the same area (rule 2, extended to everything the tile worker and the landmark packers emit): source triangles that overlap one already drawn in their plane are cut to what is not drawn yet (whatever the source says: a polygon triangulated into overlapping fans, a wall given twice, a solid given twice under two ids); a face that can never be seen (buried) is not emitted; two generated parts never land in one place (one flight of steps per spot); a simplified LOD never collapses a slot's face into another slot's plane (lock the vertices round it); foliage grown on a wall keeps off what stands in front of it (a shopfront's glass, 7.5 cm out). An integer code packed into a float vertex attribute is decoded in the vertex stage or rounded before any floor / mod / hash in the fragment stage (interpolated, it arrives with last-bit errors). Checked from the drawn geometry, at every LOD, against LOD0 for the LODs, and moving in the browser.

Adolphe Dupuich 24: a wall twice, 620 m2; Av. Hamoir 8: one solid twice, 1,940 m2); meshopt collapsed the observatory's 7 cm blue-stone plinth into its brick wall at LOD1 (1,528 m2), Saint-Pierre's surrounds (74 m2); the ivy grew over the Maison Dandoy shopfront (faceOpenings returns no opening for a shop) and its sprays crossed the shop glass. The "z-fighting" windows (images 87 / 93) were not geometry: the façade kit decoded its packed attribute codes (building hash, French window) in the fragment stage, interpolated values flipped the hashes per pixel and per TAAU jitter (gold blotches on the glass, the wall cut under a French window coming and going); the walls had had the same fix in 2026-11, the kit not.
