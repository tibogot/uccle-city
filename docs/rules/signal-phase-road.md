**Rule.** A signal approach takes its phase from the road it belongs to at its junction, never from the raw direction of a post. One table, `signalPhases(roads)` (viewer/traffic/signals.js), built from the tile's roads.json alone, is read by the traffic graph (viewer/traffic/trafficGraph.js: every signal approach, `phaseAt(node, road)`) AND the drawn posts (viewer/streets/signalPlace.js signalSites: the signal node the post's traffic meets along its road, `ahead` + `phaseAt`), so a head shows red exactly when the cars facing it stop. The table groups each signal node's arms into two axes (the most opposite pair of arms and the arms within 45 deg of it; the others), and colours the nodes (a parity union-find) so that a road linking two signal nodes under 150 m apart, through plain 2-arm nodes, is on one phase at both ends: close pairs run as one system, a green wave of offset 0. A junction whose signals are mapped on its approaches (traffic_signals up to 40 m out on two or more arms, none on the junction node) is grouped the same way and linked to them. Each cluster's orientation is the one agreeing most with the old direction rule (B north-south). An odd ring of links cannot be coloured with two phases: its least link (a one-way side street before a two-way road, then the longer link) is left off phase; 1 such ring in the city (E1 Avenue Coghen / Rue Vanderkindere / Ch. d'Alsemberg), a ceiling that never goes up. No green-wave offset other than 0: the lights have two city-wide phases (A, and B = A + half the 54 s cycle); a two-way road gets a green wave both ways only with an offset of 0 or half a cycle (the offset one way is minus the other's), and the links are under 150 m (<= 18 s at 30 km/h, inside the 22 s green), so 0 serves both directions; another offset would need per-junction head, walk and sim timings.

**Why.** The phase of a post was phaseOfDir of the nearest road segment of its OSM point (B where |uz| > |ux|), and every approach took the phase of the post it passes. Where a road bends, or runs near 45 deg, two posts of the same road a few metres apart fell on different sides of the rule: the through movement between close junctions was on two phases (trafmix: n140271967 <-> n2448080511 on Rue du Wagon both ways, n30151287 -> n254921174 on Avenue Brugmann / De Fre; 5 of 54 through movements city-wide), so a car released by the first green met the second red inside the first junction, and a queue at the second stood in the first one's turn. At the D7 Ch. d'Alsemberg junction n150588281 the signals are mapped on the approaches only, each was phased on its own, and crossing streams arrived together at the uncontrolled centre (the yield inside a turn, gap trafmix.stuck cell -1350, 1250).
