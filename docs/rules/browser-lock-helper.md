**Rule.** Every tool that drives the shared city tab or starts its own Chrome takes `browser.lock` through the one helper `tools/gate/lock.mjs` (tools/perf/allocBudget.mjs, allocSample.mjs, gpuTrace.mjs; agents by hand: `node tools/gate/lock.mjs browser hold --owner <job>` / `release --owner <job>`). (1) A lock whose holder's pid is alive and whose heartbeat (rewritten every minute) is under 10 min old is never taken, overwritten or deleted. (2) Stale = the pid is dead, or no heartbeat for > 10 min (hung, or the pid reused); a legacy lock with no pid (an older per-agent script) only after 120 min with no refresh. A stale lock is taken over under a guard file after a re-read, and logged in `<lock>.log`. (3) A child of a holder (same owner via UCCLE_LOCK_OWNER, or its parent's pid in the lock) nests. (4) A row that finds the lock busy past its wait exits 76 (LOCK_BUSY_EXIT); mergeGate re-runs it alone after the other rows (GATE_BUSY_RETRIES 3, GATE_BUSY_PAUSE_S 120 apart) and, still busy, lists it as BUSY and exits 76: never a FAIL. merge.mjs then stops without pushing and says which rows to run.

**Why.** 2026-11-28: (a) allocBudget's own lock code took any browser.lock older than 20 min by overwriting it, while xmasperf4 still held it for a timing run; (b) an agent's acquire took over a 38-min-old lock that was still in use (age alone said nothing about the holder); (c) the allocbudget row waited 25 min for a busy lock and then FAILED the whole merge, which was only a busy lock.
