**Rule.** No traffic-light approach carries more than 0.9 of its green-time capacity at the peak. The capacity is 1,800 veh/h per lane (the base saturation flow of the Highway Capacity Manual, HCM 6th ed. ch. 19: 1,900 pc/h/ln base, about 1,800 for an urban lane with heavy vehicles and turns) times the green share of the signal cycle (22 s green of 54 s). The spawned density on such a lane is capped accordingly: k <= 0.9 x 1,800 x g / C / v at its speed limit (viewer/traffic/trafficSim.js laneDensity). The data hold no traffic counts of Uccle at 8 h. Signal junctions closer than 150 m along a road share the one city-wide cycle (viewer/traffic/signals.js CYCLE). The through movement between them is on the same phase at both: a green wave with offset 0, the link run inside one green. Per-junction offsets would need per-junction light heads (the drawn lights use two city-wide phases). Baseline at the street4 merge: 3 through movements on different phases (n140271967 <-> n2448080511 both ways at D7, n30151287 -> n254921174); claude/signals1 took them to 0: the phase comes from the road at the junction (rule signal-phase-road, signals.js signalPhases, one table for the posts AND the traffic); no offset other than 0 is possible with the two city-wide phases (see that rule).

**Why.** At the peak, the 8 h density on the Ch. d'Alsemberg arterials (24-26 cars / km / lane) was more than a 22 s green passes. Queues grew without bound and locked the close D6 / D7 junctions (trafmix: service vehicles standing for good).
