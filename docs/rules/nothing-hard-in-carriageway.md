**Rule.** Nothing hard stands in a drivable carriageway: every driven lane / junction turn and every driven street's centre line is walked with the car's body against ALL the car's colliders (worker, street, gardens AND the hero models' LOD0), city-wide, in the merge gate. A collider is built from the PLACE, never from the player's state: a height band ("0.30-1.30 m over the drive surface") is measured from that place's own surface layers (viewer/drive/surfaceLayers.js), never from a function that answers for the car (driveMode.ground: the layer nearest the car); on a deck only the deck (and the street joining it level) is the surface, never the cutting / ground beside or under it; a collider found against one layer carries the height that lets a car on another layer pass. Anything placed from a sensor that does not see the object itself (a LiDAR canopy peak for a trunk) is checked against what is DRAWN as carriageway, also where no surface is measured (round 2). The load-time / build-time prop move (viewer/streets/carriageway.js offLane) never gives up inside a lane while a kerb or raised free spot is within 16 m (FAR_REACH), and a pole kept in place because the data says it stands on a tram platform is kept only where a platform is DRAWN under it (viewer/streets/tramPlatforms.js platformUnflag; round 3, claude/defects1).

**Why.** the Verrewinkel hero bridge's car blockers were built once, in a poll, from driveMode.ground (the layer nearest wherever the car was) and from "the lowest surface within 1 m", which at the deck's edge is the railway cutting 4-7 m down: the abutments, spandrels, slab and road surface became walls with no height across Rue de Verrewinkel at and on the bridge, 136 m of lanes (owner playtest 2026-11-18: "its like everywhere its blocked"); the collider audit (rule 25) left the hero models out and nothing drove every street (claude/drivecheck); round 2: 63 LiDAR trunks stood in drawn streets with no measured surface (clos, drèves): the pipeline's tree filter only knew the UrbIS surfaces

**Why (round 3, claude/defects1, 2026-11-24).** 41 props still stood in driven lanes after the snap: 22 STIB stop poles data/stops had put on an OSM tram platform line (`platform: true`), which main.js kept wherever they were, also where the fitted platform (claude/tramfit) no longer lies under them or none is in the tile (Brugmann / Churchill / Legrand: 5 in a lane; the census tested the raw OSM platforms, not the drawn ones, so 18 poles that ARE on a drawn platform were listed too); and 17 signs / plate posts / benches whose OSM node sits on the way's centre inside a wide junction roadway (no kerb within 6 m, no free spot within 8 m: offLane kept them in the lane).
