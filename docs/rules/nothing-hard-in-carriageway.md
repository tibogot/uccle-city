**Rule.** Nothing hard stands in a drivable carriageway: every driven lane / junction turn and every driven street's centre line is walked with the car's body against ALL the car's colliders (worker, street, gardens AND the hero models' LOD0), city-wide, in the merge gate. A collider is built from the PLACE, never from the player's state: a height band ("0.30-1.30 m over the drive surface") is measured from that place's own surface layers (viewer/drive/surfaceLayers.js), never from a function that answers for the car (driveMode.ground: the layer nearest the car); on a deck only the deck (and the street joining it level) is the surface, never the cutting / ground beside or under it; a collider found against one layer carries the height that lets a car on another layer pass. Anything placed from a sensor that does not see the object itself (a LiDAR canopy peak for a trunk) is checked against what is DRAWN as carriageway, also where no surface is measured (round 2).
