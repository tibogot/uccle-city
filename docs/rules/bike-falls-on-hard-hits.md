**Rule.** The kid's BMX falls only on a HARD hit, as rule 40 asks of the motorbike: head-on (within 53 deg of square) into a wall / car / post / tree faster than crashSpeed (4.8 m/s), a face higher than the wheel climbs (stepClimb 19 cm) square at speed, a landing far off (nose down past 55 deg - less after a hard impact: a 2 m drop onto the front wheel bails at ~36 deg -, sideways past 46 deg, leaning past 49 deg, a 5 m flat drop), a trick still held, a real slide (2 m/s sideways for 0.45 s, a skid or a locked front). Kerbs (up / down, square to 60 deg), cobbles, roots, bumps, sand, pump rollers, small drops and slightly crooked landings are ridden through (at most a wobble or a "sketchy" landing). The assists act only without the player's input (a spin needs the steer pressed IN the air, Z / X override the air level). Skill stays scored: a hop keeps the speed a roll-up loses, a crouched landing is cleaner. Proven headless by a casual keyboard rider over many seeds on every lab spot with every crash logged with its cause (the table in docs/BIKE.md).

**Why.** owner playtest 2026-11-25 (the BMX lab): "it's cool but it's too easy to fall. With the BMX we should fall only if we hit something hard, but it should go easily over bumps and all." tools/bike/casualRide.mjs on the old tuning: 1.49 crashes a minute (206 in 840 rides; 2.26 with a twitchier steering): the front wheel into every kerb face over 3.2 m/s (147, square or at 45 deg), "landed sideways" after small hops (40-146: the steer key still held from the ground or a correction tap spun the bike in the air, and the yaw rate kept turning it after touch-down), washed out turning in sand (7-12: the rolling resistance took the tyre's side grip, the handlebar turn was not grip-limited), looped out / endo off drops and bonks (the rear bonk threw the bike nose down), walls hit at a glance. After: 0.05 a minute, only real hits on the plaza (claude/kid)
