**Rule.** ONE ground for every mover: the traffic (driving, knocked, wrecks), the parked cars and two-wheelers, the trams and anything else that moves or stands on a street (the props' bodies) stand on the same function the player's car drives on (viewer/drive/moverGround.js moverGroundY: the road decks, then the drawn drive surface, the layer nearest the mover's own height where two stack), never on the terrain + a constant or another derived height; a new mover with no height yet is not placed where two layers stack. A height sampled from a raster is never a ramp across a step the mover cannot climb (> 0.5 m inside a cell: the mover's side only) nor a kerb blended under a parked wheel; a deck's walked top never lies under its drawn slab; trams and their rails on a road deck lie on its drawn top. The lookup is a raster / deck test (no raycast), no allocation, cheap enough for every wheel of every traffic car every step. Checked against the DRAWN street (its triangles), not against the drive grid.

Carsoel deck (H6), on Place de Saint-Job by Botanea / Pizza Hut and in front of the Pasta Commedia: main.js movingGround = the decks, else worldRef.surface + CARVE, while the player's car drove the drawn drive surface; wherever the street is drawn off the carved terrain + 12 cm (the levelled carriageways and squares of rules 31 / 38, the street drawn on a deck over its fitted top) the traffic, tram 92 and the parked cars sank or floated; then the census found the Carsoel plan deck walked 13 cm over its slab (no drawn-top grid for a band-fitted deck), a car 0.5 m from a 1.6 m terrace riding the 1 m grid's ramp 1.4 m up (I6), parked kerb-side wheels 5-10 cm up (the grid's kerb blend) and the trams 0.1-0.2 m into the Carsoel deck (the track profile interpolated between the bridge ends) (claude/ground, docs/MOVERGROUND.md)
