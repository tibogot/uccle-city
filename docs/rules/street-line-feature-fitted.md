**Rule.** A line feature that runs IN the street (a tram track; anything laid along the carriageway from another source than the street survey) is fitted to the MEASURED street as drawn before it is drawn: both rails (the full gauge) clear of every raised class (sidewalk, median, square, path, ramp, tree pit) and >= 0.2 m from every drawn kerb line, two tracks never closer than min(their source spacing, 2.75 m), the move smooth (|o'| <= 0.05, |o''| <= 1/300 m: no new bend under the real 18 m minimum radius beyond the listed spots) and continuous across tile seams (fitted once for the whole line, cut into tiles after); where it truly crosses paving (a kerb at > 20 deg to it: a site entrance, a terminus loop) it is left as the source draws it and listed. Everything that reads the line reads the fitted one, and what stands beside it is placed from the fitted line (a tram platform's edge 1.25 m from the track centre, never over a rail; what stands on the platform moves with it); checked city-wide on what is drawn, and the trams' path on it.

**Why.** the OSM tram ways were drawn as they are (viewer/track.js only rounded them) and nothing held them to the UrbIS street: a track 0.3 m from the tram road's kerb (Ch. d'Alsemberg, owner image 92), 3.7 m spacing on Av. Churchill's 4.75 m UrbIS tram site; the rail top = max(drawn surface, profile) climbed onto the paving: 48 runs, 421 m of rail on raised paving + 152 m in the kerb line (census class E, owner playtest 2026-11-18); and 705 m of rail ran under an OSM platform slab (tram.js: a band centred on an OSM line that is often the platform's edge) (claude/tramfit)
