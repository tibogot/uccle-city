**Rule.** A character never gets its look from a flat base colour and one roughness per slot. Every material slot carries relief (baked: seams, hems, ribbing, folds, the zip; plus a tiling micro weave / knit / twill / pore sheet in the bind pose), a roughness map (oily vs matte zones, rubber vs leather), cavity, and its own response: cloth has sheen and a low specular, skin a wrapped, reddened diffuse and warmer cheeks / nose / ears / lips, hair a strand highlight and a soft hairline, eyes a catchlight. The vertex or baked AO goes into the material's `aoNode` (it must occlude the sky light AND the sky's reflection), not only into the colour. No additive rim and no emissive "glow" on skin or cloth (they shine the same in the shade). The maps are baked by script (tools/blender/kidLookBake.py) in the atlas layout and packed to KTX2 (tools/kid/kidLookPack.py); the old look stays behind `?kidlook=old` until the owner OKs.

**Why.** Kid A v4 (owner 2026-11-28: "looks too much like plastic"). Root cause, found in viewer/kid/kidModel.js kidAtlasMaterial and the pack: (1) the MPFB normal maps were dropped at the pack and the scripted garments (jacket, socks, cap, brims) were flat colour cells, so nothing broke up the highlights; (2) one roughness per slot (the jacket at 0.5 with the full dielectric F0) gave one smooth, even sheen over every garment; (3) the AO only multiplied the colour, so the sky's reflection still lit every crease; (4) an additive rim (+10 % of the base at every silhouette) and a skin emissive lit the kid evenly, even in the shade: the "painted plastic toy" read. The mesh was not the problem (the look round adds no triangle and keeps one draw per LOD). GPU in the bike lab, face close-up, page in front: 4.28 ms/frame for both looks (old 4.284, new 4.289 / 4.274 ms over 480 frames each).
