**Rule.** A character never gets its look from a flat base colour and one roughness per slot, and never from maps alone. Every material slot carries relief (baked: seams, hems, ribbing, folds, the zip; plus a tiling micro weave / knit / twill / pore sheet in the bind pose), a roughness map (oily vs matte zones, rubber vs leather), cavity, and its own response: cloth has sheen and a low specular, skin a wrapped, reddened diffuse and warmer cheeks / nose / ears / lips, hair a strand highlight and a soft hairline, eyes a catchlight. The vertex or baked AO goes into the material's `aoNode` (it must occlude the sky light AND the sky's reflection), not only into the colour, but never so hard that he reads darker than the scene round him (round 2: the colour floor 0.97, the sky occluded to 0.8 at most, 0.95 on the skin). No additive rim and no emissive "glow" on skin or cloth (they shine the same in the shade). **The clothes are shapes too**: every garment has real thickness where its edge shows (hems, cuffs, the collar, the cap's sweatband: a thicker solidify band behind a support loop, a separate hem loop for a later cloth sim), a hem that stands off the body where the garment does (the blouson over the elastic), the shorts a waistband, pocket openings and a turned-up cuff, the socks a ribbed top, the sneakers a sole lip, laces and a tongue, a brim with enough segments and weighted normals; every garment's atlas cell is 2-D (u round the limb, v along it) so its folds are baked, never a 1-D colour strip; the hair has volume (an opaque shell with chunky locks under the cards, no overdraw stack of transparent cards); the face's shape (eyes, brows, nose, lips, ears) is tuned with the body's own shape targets, never painted on. The maps are baked by script (tools/blender/kidLookBake.py) in the atlas layout and packed to KTX2 (tools/kid/kidLookPack.py); the shapes are scripted in tools/blender/kidGame.py (round 2, `--r1` rebuilds round 1); the looks before stay behind `?kidlook=r1` / `?kidlook=old` until the owner OKs.

**Why.** Kid A v4 (owner 2026-11-28: "looks too much like plastic"). Root cause round 1, found in viewer/kid/kidModel.js kidAtlasMaterial and the pack: (1) the MPFB normal maps were dropped at the pack and the scripted garments (jacket, socks, cap, brims) were flat colour cells, so nothing broke up the highlights; (2) one roughness per slot (the jacket at 0.5 with the full dielectric F0) gave one smooth, even sheen over every garment; (3) the AO only multiplied the colour, so the sky's reflection still lit every crease; (4) an additive rim (+10 % of the base at every silhouette) and a skin emissive lit the kid evenly, even in the shade: the "painted plastic toy" read. GPU in the bike lab, face close-up, page in front: 4.28 ms/frame for both looks. Round 2 (same day, the owner wanted better clothes, skin, face and hair): round 1 fixed the shading but left the SHAPES of a shrink-wrapped mannequin: every garment was the body's own surface pushed out by a constant (a 4 mm shell with a paper edge), the sleeves / socks / cap had 1-D colour strips so no fold could be baked into them, the brim had 17 x 4 segments (facets in a grazing light), the hair was a few cards pressed flat under the cap, and the full AO on the sky light made him darker than the old look overall. Found on the way: a 2 cm fold wave displaced into a ~1.2 cm mesh aliased into a crumpled zig-zag (only long shapes go into the mesh, the short folds are baked); a separate 3 mm lip under the shorts' cuff made long thin triangles that folded (kidTest's spike row); single scalp vertices left among the hidden ones under the hair shell made 12-tri skin islands (kidTest's stray-piece row); straight lace bars sank into the curved upper (laces follow the shoe's top, ray-cast per section). Review fix: the cap's white panel was painted narrowing up the crown while its seams were baked at a constant angle from the bind position, so the seam cavity slid onto the white, and a baked sweatband stitch drew a hard line across it; a garment's baked seams are placed from the SAME coordinate the colour cell is painted in (the cell's own u), and a colour edge never takes a darkening groove on its light side.
