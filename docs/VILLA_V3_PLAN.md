## 1. What is there today (inventory)

Everything is generated by **one script**, `tools/blender/villa.py` (131 kB), with the shared furniture helpers from
`tools/blender/interior_kit.py` (`soft`, `box`, `lathe`, `plant`). `tools/villa/pack.mjs` packs the result. The script
builds every item from primitives:

- `K.soft`: a subdivided cube pushed onto a rounded box, 12 x max(n)^2 triangles.
- `K.box`: a bevelled box.
- `K.lathe`: a turned profile.

Repeated items are merged into **one primitive per material slot**, so the GLBs hold no per-item meshes. The per-item
triangle counts below are computed from the generator's parameters.

**There are no textures anywhere.** Both GLBs have 0 images and 0 textures. Every colour is a TSL formula in the viewer:
a constant colour plus value noise, with no normal, roughness or AO maps. Interior light is **baked per vertex** (sky,
sun and night, `_LSKY` / `_LSUN` / `_LNIGHT`) on a 0.42 m densify grid (`MI`). Contact shadows under furniture are
therefore at most as sharp as that grid. There is no AO texture and no runtime light (rule fixed-light-count).

## 2. What makes it look cheap (judged against the premium bar + real references)

The bar: the project's Blender quality bar (bevels, AO, real detail, no bad shading from topology, flat normals on
planes, weighted normals) and realistic-first named-slot materials. The references are real modern luxury villa
interiors: Minotti, B&B Italia and Poliform catalogue rooms, Belgian architect villas in Uccle / Rhode-Saint-Genèse on
Street View. These were looked at only; nothing was stored or traced.

**Cross-cutting causes**, which explain about 70 % of the "quick first round" look:

1. **No surface detail at all.** No texture, normal map or roughness on any material. Fabric is a colour plus blotchy
   value noise, so it reads as plastic or suede. Leather reads as wood veneer: the bench and throw in i07 show a
   large-scale noise that looks like burl. Glass and ceramic are matte.
2. **Unlit interior shading with no specular.** The bakes carry diffuse light only. A sheen is faked only on brass,
   metal, stone and marble (a fresnel add). Nothing glossy reflects: the TV, the bottles, the mirror (pure black, i08)
   and the lacquered or polished surfaces.
3. **Contact shadows at 0.42 m vertex resolution.** Sofas, beds, chairs and tables do not sit on the floor; they hover
   with a soft grey smudge at best (i04: the sofa's plinth line). Real interiors read as solid because of the dark
   2-5 cm crease where objects meet the floor and the wall.
4. **Soft goods are inflated boxes.** `K.soft` has no seams, piping, welts, creases, sag or drape. Every cushion is the
   same pillow shape.
5. **Lamps don't light.** At night (i04_night, i06_night) a lit table lamp leaves no warm pool on the wall or table.
   The night bake is flat.
6. **The rooms are under-dressed for a luxury house.** There are no curtains, no glassware, no objects on tables, no
   throws over the sofa arms. The kitchen has no sink, tap, hob or appliances. The bathroom has no toilet, shower, taps
   on the tub, towels or mirror glass.

## 3. Proposed method per item

Common to all items:

- **Shared hero-props material.** One new interior slot, `props`, is a texture atlas: KTX2 (UASTC/ETC1S) base colour +
  ORM + normal, 2k, so one draw for every upgraded soft or hard item. It is lit by the same three vertex bakes. Each
  item also gets its own **baked AO + contact shadow in the atlas's ORM.R**, Cycles-baked per item at about 4 px/cm.
  This fixes cause 3 without densifying the floor. The floor under each item gets one small **contact decal quad**
  (a baked soft shadow, transparent multiply, sharing the decal draw).
- **Specular from the existing probes.** A cheap GGX lobe on the env map already in the scene, driven by ORM roughness,
  in the `props` and `glass` materials only. It is the same idea as the marble fresnel today, but read from the normal
  map. This fixes cause 2 for the hero items without a runtime light.
- **Cloth.** Scripted Blender cloth simulation (Blender 5.0, headless, OptiX for the bakes). Bedding, throws and
  cushions are simulated against the scripted frames. The result is remeshed and decimated to budget (weighted normals,
  no streak/bentflat in mesh_audit). The high-poly sim is kept for the normal and AO bake.
- **Textures.** Poly Haven / ambientCG CC0 scans for fabric weave, leather, linen and wood (licences below). They are
  re-packed into the atlas, with colour kept in named-slot tints in TSL (no baked colour beyond the scan's albedo
  detail).
- **Joins.** Every item is placed by its own collider box on the floor (`colb`). mesh_audit's contact check is extended
  to the interior props (rule joins-rule): no gap over 5 mm under feet or plinths.
