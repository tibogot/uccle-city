Uccle 3D — labs

One page per asset or system, to look at it on its own before it goes into the city. Back to the city.

Bird LabThe Saint-Job peregrine falcon: every clip, the flight blend, the procedural layers fly mode uses (dive, bank, feet, braking), lights and debug views. Falcon LabThe Saint-Job peregrine family at the real church: the eyrie behind the louvred arch (gravel tray, feathers, prey, droppings; tools/blender/falconNest.py), three downy eyases, the female and the tiercel with their behaviours (perches on the cross, gable and louvre ledges, thermals, stoops, the food pass, feeding), views from inside the belfry, the street and the sky, Wikimedia Commons reference photos side by side, CPU / GPU cost A/B. In the city by default (?falcons=0 = none); fly mode starts from the nest. Lamp LabThe Brussels swan-neck lamp from Blender (tools/blender/brusselsLamp.py): its three LODs side by side with triangle counts, the LOD a lamp gets at any distance, day and night glow. Tram LabThe STIB T3000 of tram 92 from Blender (tools/blender/tram.py): five sections articulating on an 18 m Saint-Job curve, LODs, the LED headsigns, day and night, photo overlays, 20 trams for the GPU cost. In the city by default. Town Hall LabThe Uccle town hall (Maison communale, Place Jean Vander Elst) as a scripted hero asset (tools/blender/townhall.py, from the UrbIS solid + LiDAR DSM + the photos facade by facade): brick and stone Louis XIII facades, slate mansards with dormers and oculi, portico, balcony and perron; the photo viewpoints with an overlay, LOD cross-fades, wireframe, the UrbIS solid, a live material-slot swap through the adapter table, GPU benchmark. Church LabÉglise Saint-Job as a scripted hero asset (tools/blender/stjob.py, from the UrbIS solid + LiDAR): at its real place on the real ground under the city's lighting; the reference photos' viewpoints with an overlay, LODs (auto by distance), wireframe, the UrbIS solid it will replace, the falcon nest node, GPU benchmark. Car LabThe driving model drive mode uses (handling and suspension sliders, chase camera, a test ground with walls, a courtyard, a kerb island, a ramp and posts, colliders drawn) with any car of the one loader (?car=: the scripted hatchback, Kenney or Quaternius CC0 cars). Car Lab · compareWhich cars for the city? Our scripted hatchback (tools/blender/car.py) vs the Kenney Car Kit vs the Quaternius Cars Pack (CC0, tools/cars/convertPacks.mjs), at a kerb under the city's lighting and post chain; our own faceted family (tools/blender/carFaceted.py: 7 types + a Brussels taxi, baked AO, see-through glass); a lineup of all; meshoptimizer LODs; paints; dark glass. Car Lab · street of 300304 parked cars per option along two streets: instancing per model / LOD / material, per-instance paint, LOD and culling per car; triangles, draw calls and GPU ms, A/B benchmark. Capsule LabThe third-person character (viewer/character/): a kinematic capsule (walk, run, jump, step-up onto kerbs and stairs, slopes, ground snap) on a test course of kerbs, stairs, ramps, a corridor and a 0.9 m door, gaps, low ceilings and a small house; a spring-arm camera that never goes through walls; a CC0 animated character (Quaternius) with foot IK on stairs, kerbs and ramps; tests and CPU cost. In the city: the "Third person" button. Pedestrian LabMr.doob's code-built people (vendor/slop-ped, MIT) on a test ground (sidewalks with tree pits and a detour, a signalled junction, a zebra, a square, a tram stop, a park path; test cars, a signal clock, a tram): his live rig near, a bone table baked from his gait on the GPU further out, three mesh LODs with dithered fades, nothing past 250 m; density / hour sliders, meters (foot slide, ground gap, pops, spawns in view), moving checks, CPU / GPU A/B against his all-live crowd. Lab only (docs/PEDESTRIANS.md).
