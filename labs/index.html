Uccle 3D — labs

One page per asset or system, to look at it on its own before it goes into the city. Back to the city.

Bird LabThe Saint-Job peregrine falcon: every clip, the flight blend, the procedural layers fly mode uses (dive, bank, feet, braking), lights and debug views. Lamp LabThe Brussels swan-neck lamp from Blender (tools/blender/brusselsLamp.py): its three LODs side by side with triangle counts, the LOD a lamp gets at any distance, day and night glow. Tram LabThe STIB T3000 of tram 92 from Blender (tools/blender/tram.py): five sections articulating on an 18 m Saint-Job curve, LODs, the LED headsigns, day and night, photo overlays, 20 trams for the GPU cost. In the city by default. Church LabÉglise Saint-Job as a scripted hero asset (tools/blender/stjob.py, from the UrbIS solid + LiDAR): at its real place on the real ground under the city's lighting; the reference photos' viewpoints with an overlay, LODs (auto by distance), wireframe, the UrbIS solid it will replace, the falcon nest node, GPU benchmark. Car LabThe driving model drive mode uses (handling and suspension sliders, chase camera, a test ground with walls, a courtyard, a kerb island, a ramp and posts, colliders drawn) with any car of the one loader (?car=: the scripted hatchback, Kenney or Quaternius CC0 cars). Car Lab · compareWhich cars for the city? Our scripted hatchback (tools/blender/car.py) vs the Kenney Car Kit vs the Quaternius Cars Pack (CC0, tools/cars/convertPacks.mjs), at a kerb under the city's lighting and post chain; our own faceted family (tools/blender/carFaceted.py: 7 types + a Brussels taxi, baked AO, see-through glass); a lineup of all; meshoptimizer LODs; paints; dark glass. Car Lab · street of 300304 parked cars per option along two streets: instancing per model / LOD / material, per-instance paint, LOD and culling per car; triangles, draw calls and GPU ms, A/B benchmark. Capsule LabThe third-person character (viewer/character/): a kinematic capsule (walk, run, jump, step-up onto kerbs and stairs, slopes, ground snap) on a test course of kerbs, stairs, ramps, a corridor and a 0.9 m door, gaps, low ceilings and a small house; a spring-arm camera that never goes through walls; a CC0 animated character (Quaternius) with foot IK on stairs, kerbs and ramps; tests and CPU cost. In the city: the "Third person" button.
Tree LabThe stylized street trees: species, LODs, wind, impostors, GPU cost per tree.
Material LabThe shared material library (viewer/materials/: 22 named TSL materials for the city, the hero buildings and the façade kit) on a test gallery (wall with a window and sill, corner, plinth, kerb, roof, dome, tower) under the city's lighting: procedural vs CC0 scan (KTX2) vs hybrid side by side, 1 m to 300 m, every parameter live, GPU ms / shader size / texture memory, save choices.json, and a city sample: today's city shaders vs the library.
