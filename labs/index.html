Uccle 3D — labs

One page per asset or system, to look at it on its own before it goes into the city. Back to the city.

Bird LabThe Saint-Job peregrine falcon: every clip, the flight blend, the procedural layers fly mode uses (dive, bank, feet, braking), lights and debug views. Falcon LabThe Saint-Job peregrine family at the real church: the eyrie behind the louvred arch (gravel tray, feathers, prey, droppings; tools/blender/falconNest.py), three downy eyases, the female and the tiercel with their behaviours (perches on the cross, gable and louvre ledges, thermals, stoops, the food pass, feeding), views from inside the belfry, the street and the sky, Wikimedia Commons reference photos side by side, CPU / GPU cost A/B. In the city by default (?falcons=0 = none); fly mode starts from the nest. Map LabThe minimap (round, heading-up, upright street names, current + next street from the road graph, tram 92, landmarks) and the full map (M: pan, wheel / pinch zoom, bilingual search, click to teleport) over a player walking, driving or flying along real Uccle streets, on a day / dusk / night sky; CPU per frame and worker cache rebuilds. Baked by tools/build_map.py; in the city behind ?map=1. Falcon Flight LabFly as a peregrine over Saint-Job (lab only, next to today's fly mode): a semi-physical flight model (lift and drag, spread / half-folded / tucked wings, a forgiving stall, 80° banks, 7 g pull-outs, flapping from a stamina budget), stoops past 80 m/s, the wind with gusts, thermals over the Place and the parks (gulls circling, dust rising), updrafts off the façades, a real collision; pigeons (boids, vertex-shader wings) that burst off the roofs, the strike and catch window, carrying prey to the eyrie, landing with a flare on the church's cross and ledges; chase / head camera, FOV and streaks with speed, procedural wind and wing sound; keyboard, mouse aim, gamepad, touch; verification autopilots and traces. Lamp LabThe Brussels swan-neck lamp from Blender (tools/blender/brusselsLamp.py): its three LODs side by side with triangle counts, the LOD a lamp gets at any distance, day and night glow. Flag LabFlags on poles (viewer/flags/): a Verlet cloth with wind for the few nearest flags, a recorded loop played by the GPU for all the others (one draw per design, zero CPU), hand-overs both ways without a pop; the town hall's three balcony poles (EU / Belgium / Uccle) with wall collision, masts, a field of up to 600 flags for the cost; wind, fabric, size, live vs baked. Stalle LabThe Chapelle de Stalle (Notre-Dame de Bon-Secours, rue de Stalle 50; Gothic, end of the 15th century) and the 1711 sacristan's house as a scripted hero asset (tools/blender/stalle.py, from the UrbIS solids + LiDAR DSM + photos counted facade by facade): rubble sandstone, tracery, oculi, the hexagonal bell-cote; photo viewpoints with solved camera poses and an overlay, LOD cross-fades, wireframe, the UrbIS solids, a live material-slot swap, GPU benchmark; and its INTERIOR cell (tools/blender/stalle_interior.py: nave on round piers, two tiny aisles, the coved stucco ceiling, the Baroque retable; Cycles-baked sky / sun / night light): walk in with the third-person character. Château LabThe Château de Wolvendael (18th c., today the École d’art d’Uccle) as a scripted hero asset (tools/blender/wolvendael_chateau.py, from the UrbIS solids + LiDAR DSM + the photos’ solved camera poses, facade by facade): white render, rusticated stone quoins and strips, the three arched French windows, louvred shutters, the slate mansard with its dormers, zinc rolls and slate-hung chimneys; and ITS POND, the park-water system (viewer/water/): the bed carved in the DTM, calm water with a planar reflection, reeds and lilies. Photo overlays, LOD cross-fades, slot swap, GPU benchmark (château and pond apart), flicker test.
