Bird LabThe Saint-Job peregrine falcon: every clip, the flight blend, the procedural layers fly mode uses (dive, bank, feet, braking), lights and debug views. Falcon LabThe Saint-Job peregrine family at the real church: the eyrie behind the louvred arch (gravel tray, feathers, prey, droppings; tools/blender/falconNest.py), three downy eyases, the female and the tiercel with their behaviours (perches on the cross, gable and louvre ledges, thermals, stoops, the food pass, feeding), views from inside the belfry, the street and the sky, Wikimedia Commons reference photos side by side, CPU / GPU cost A/B. In the city by default (?falcons=0 = none); fly mode starts from the nest. Lamp LabThe Brussels swan-neck lamp from Blender (tools/blender/brusselsLamp.py): its three LODs side by side with triangle counts, the LOD a lamp gets at any distance, day and night glow. Tram LabThe STIB T3000 of tram 92 from Blender (tools/blender/tram.py): five sections articulating on an 18 m Saint-Job curve, LODs, the LED headsigns, day and night, photo overlays, 20 trams for the GPU cost. In the city by default. Church LabÉglise Saint-Job as a scripted hero asset (tools/blender/stjob.py, from the UrbIS solid + LiDAR): at its real place on the real ground under the city's lighting; the reference photos' viewpoints with an overlay, LODs (auto by distance), wireframe, the UrbIS solid it will replace, the falcon nest node, GPU benchmark. Capsule LabThe third-person character (viewer/character/): a kinematic capsule (walk, run, jump, step-up onto kerbs and stairs, slopes, ground snap) on a test course of kerbs, stairs, ramps, a corridor and a 0.9 m door, gaps, low ceilings and a small house; a spring-arm camera that never goes through walls; a CC0 animated character (Quaternius) with foot IK on stairs, kerbs and ramps; tests and CPU cost.
